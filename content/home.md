### Madar

#### Performance and workflow improvements in a growing logistics platform

Madar is a team-built logistics platform that grew to approximately 8,000–10,000 daily shipments,

## Open source

### `saudi-utils`

Saudi applications repeatedly need to validate structured values such as identity, banking,
business, tax, telecom, and National Address data. `saudi-utils` provides typed, deterministic
offline checks while stating clearly what those checks cannot prove.

The first published version has no runtime dependencies. During the 17 September 2026 audit, its
1,503 tests passed with 100% measured statement, branch, function, and line coverage. Those figures
describe that audited version; they do not prove that every rule is permanently correct or that an
offline result confirms a real-world identity, account, or registration.

**Actions:** [View the source](https://github.com/alialaraby/saudi-utils) ·
[Open the npm package](https://www.npmjs.com/package/saudi-utils) · Read the case study →
`/work/saudi-utils`
